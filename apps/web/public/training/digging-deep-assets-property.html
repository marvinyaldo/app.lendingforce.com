DISCOVERY AREA 03

Assets

Asset questions reveal far more than a bank balance. They surface the borrower's financial habits, their comfort level, and any upcoming events that could change how — or whether — they qualify.

ASSETS DISCOVERY

Financially Stable

DOCUMENT FULLY · FIND THE EXTRAS

WHY IT MATTERS

Stable borrowers qualify easily, but the file still needs full asset documentation. Ask about retirement, upcoming lump sums, and the cushion they want to protect.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1Where do you primarily bank — do you keep accounts in one place or spread out?
  • 2Do you have a savings cushion you like to maintain — an amount you'd rather not drop below?
  • 3Are there retirement or investment accounts we should be aware of for reserves?
  • 4Any upcoming bonuses, tax refunds, or lump sums we should factor in?
  • 5Would you rather keep as much cash as possible after closing, or are you comfortable using most of it?

ASSETS DISCOVERY

Feeling Stretched

FIND CREATIVE PATHS TO QUALIFY

WHY IT MATTERS

Cash-tight borrowers often need creative options — gift funds, down-payment assistance, seller credits, or lender-paid comp. Understand the situation before you propose a fix.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1When you say things have been tight — is that recent, or has it been building for a while?
  • 2Do you have access to any gift funds or family help for the down payment?
  • 3Are there retirement accounts we could count for reserves, even if you'd rather not touch them?
  • 4Do you have any income changes coming up — a raise, a new job, or side income starting?
  • 5Would rolling costs into the loan, or using lender credits to offset them, be helpful?

ASSETS DISCOVERY

Retirement & Investments

RESERVES — AND WHAT THEY'LL USE

WHY IT MATTERS

Investment accounts can count toward reserves, but they need context. Learn what the borrower is willing to use versus what they want to preserve.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1Do you have a 401(k), IRA, or investment accounts we could count for reserves?
  • 2Are you fully vested, or is some of it employer-matched and not yet accessible?
  • 3Are you comfortable with those funds being counted on paper, or would you rather not list them?
  • 4Would you consider liquidating any for the down payment, or do you want to leave them untouched?
  • 5Do you hold any stocks or mutual funds in a taxable brokerage account?

ASSETS DISCOVERY

Incoming Funds

MUST BE SOURCED & SEASONED

WHY IT MATTERS

Incoming funds must be documented, sourced, and seasoned before they can be used. Establish what's coming, when it lands, and where it's from.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1When are you expecting that bonus or deposit — and roughly how much are we talking?
  • 2Is it already in your account, or is it still coming in?
  • 3For gift funds: is this from a family member, and would they sign a gift letter?
  • 4Are you expecting a tax refund that might help with closing costs?
  • 5Would you be comfortable timing the application to line up with those funds hitting your account?

DISCOVERY AREA 04

Property

Property questions surface the equity position, the borrower's plans for the home, and any appraisal issues to anticipate. The long-term plan decides whether a rate reduction or a cash-out makes more financial sense.

PROPERTY DISCOVERY

Long-Term Home

POINTS & UPFRONT COSTS PAY OFF

WHY IT MATTERS

Borrowers staying 5+ years benefit most from rate reductions, PMI removal, and equity moves. With a long horizon, the math favors paying points or costs upfront.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1How long have you owned the home, and what's changed since you bought it?
  • 2Have you done any improvements or renovations that might have increased the value?
  • 3Do you have a rough sense of what it's worth today?
  • 4With a long-term horizon, would a slightly higher upfront cost be worth a meaningfully lower rate?
  • 5Is the goal to lower the payment, shorten the term, or pull some equity out?

PROPERTY DISCOVERY

May Move Soon

LOW-COST / NO-COST MAKES SENSE

WHY IT MATTERS

If they may move in 1–3 years, they need to recoup costs quickly. Lender-paid comp or a no-cost refinance often beats paying points.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1What's your rough timeline — are we talking 1–2 years or 3–5 years?
  • 2Is there a reason for the potential move — a new job, family, or upsizing?
  • 3If the lender covered most closing costs through the rate, would that change the math for you?
  • 4Have you thought about renting the home instead of selling if you move?
  • 5Is the main goal to reduce monthly costs now, even if you might sell before fully recouping?

PROPERTY DISCOVERY

Rental / Investment

DIFFERENT LTV, RATES & DOCS

WHY IT MATTERS

Investment properties carry different LTV limits, higher rates, and rental-income documentation requirements. Get the full picture before you structure anything.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1Is this currently rented, and do you have a lease agreement in place?
  • 2Are you reporting the rental income on your tax returns?
  • 3What's the monthly rent versus the mortgage payment — is it cash-flowing positively?
  • 4Is this the only investment property, or do you have others we should be aware of?
  • 5Are you looking to pull cash out, lower the rate, or both?

PROPERTY DISCOVERY

Value Unknown

PIN DOWN VALUE FIRST

WHY IT MATTERS

Help the borrower ballpark value before getting too far in. A quick market check or appraisal confirms whether the deal even pencils out.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1Do you know what similar homes in your neighborhood have been selling for lately?
  • 2Have you done any major updates since you bought it — kitchens, baths, additions?
  • 3What did you pay originally, and do you think it's gone up or down since then?
  • 4Have any neighbors sold recently that gave you a ballpark?
  • 5Would it help if I pulled recent sales data for your area to give us a starting point?

What to Carry Into Every Call

1

Assets tell a story, not just a balance.

How and where someone keeps their money reveals their habits, comfort level, and the cushion they'll protect.

2

Separate what counts from what they'll use.

Reserves on paper aren't the same as cash a borrower will actually part with. Ask which is which.

3

Match the strategy to the timeline.

Long-term stayers can pay points; likely movers need low-cost or no-cost structures to recoup quickly.

4

Pin down value and documentation early.

A ballpark value, plus sourced and seasoned funds, turns an uncertain file into one you can structure.
