DISCOVERY AREA 01

Goals

Start with the surface reason, then dig deeper. The stated goal is rarely the whole story. A borrower who says "lower my payment" may really need cash-flow relief from job stress or family expenses. Your job is to find the reason underneath the reason.

GOALS DISCOVERY

Lower the Monthly Payment

DIG BENEATH THE STATED GOAL

WHY IT MATTERS

"Lower my payment" is a surface answer. Find out whether it's about a fixed budget, freeing up flexibility, or a specific life change behind it.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1What would having a lower payment actually change for you day-to-day?
  • 2Is this more about the monthly savings, or is there something specific the difference would go toward?
  • 3Are you on a fixed budget right now, or is this more about freeing up flexibility?
  • 4Have you had any changes to your income or expenses recently?
  • 5How long are you planning to stay in this home — long-term, or possibly selling in a few years?

GOALS DISCOVERY

Cash Out & Debt Consolidation

UNDERSTAND THE USE OF FUNDS

WHY IT MATTERS

Cash-out goals usually have a specific purpose. Knowing the use of funds lets you size the loan and present the right product.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1What would the cash be used for — home improvements, debt payoff, an emergency fund?
  • 2How much debt are we talking about — do you have a rough total in mind?
  • 3If we could consolidate that debt and lower your overall payment, would that feel like a win?
  • 4Do you have a specific amount you need to pull out, or are you open to exploring what makes sense?
  • 5What's driving the timing — is there something coming up that prompted this?

GOALS DISCOVERY

PMI Removal & Term Change

USUALLY AN EASIER CONVERSATION

WHY IT MATTERS

Borrowers focused on PMI or term are often equity-aware and financially engaged — a great signal. Confirm the value picture and expand the goal.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1Do you know roughly what your home is worth today compared to when you bought it?
  • 2Have you made any improvements that might have increased the value?
  • 3What made you start thinking about PMI removal now — did you notice it on your statement?
  • 4If we could remove the PMI and keep your payment similar, or even lower it, would that interest you?
  • 5Are you also open to talking about shortening the term if the numbers made sense?

GOALS DISCOVERY

Still Exploring

FIND THE EMOTIONAL TRIGGER

WHY IT MATTERS

Borrowers who are "just exploring" usually have a trigger they haven't fully put into words yet. Your job is to surface it.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1What made you decide to look into your options now — was there something specific that prompted it?
  • 2Is there a financial goal you've had on the back burner that this might help with?
  • 3When you think about your mortgage, what's the one thing you'd change if you could?
  • 4Are you more focused on reducing your monthly cost, accessing some equity, or both?
  • 5What would the ideal outcome look like if everything went perfectly?

DISCOVERY AREA 02

Income

Income type decides which questions matter most. W-2 salaried is the simplest. Hourly and commission need a two-year history and average. Self-employed requires returns and a real look at the business. Match your questions to the type in front of you.

INCOME DISCOVERY

Salaried / W-2 Employee

FOCUS ON STABILITY & TENURE

WHY IT MATTERS

The simplest income to document. Confirm tenure, the makeup of the pay, and anything on the horizon that could shift qualification.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1How long have you been with your current employer?
  • 2Is your income base salary, or does it include any bonuses or overtime?
  • 3Has your income changed recently — a raise, a reduction, or a change in hours?
  • 4How secure do you feel in your position right now?
  • 5Are you planning to stay in this role, or are there any upcoming changes?

INCOME DISCOVERY

Hourly / Commission

NEEDS 2-YEAR HISTORY & AVERAGE

WHY IT MATTERS

Variable income is qualified on a two-year average. Probe consistency, recent trend, and how the pay is actually structured.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1Would you say your income has been consistent over the last two years, or has it fluctuated?
  • 2Do you work overtime regularly, and do you expect that to continue?
  • 3How does your commission structure work — tied to deals, hours, or performance?
  • 4Has your income gone up or down recently compared to prior years?
  • 5Do you have pay stubs and W-2s from the last two years handy?

INCOME DISCOVERY

Self-Employed / Business Owner

NEEDS 2 YRS RETURNS + BUSINESS DOCS

WHY IT MATTERS

Qualified from tax returns and business documentation. Understanding the business helps you explain how the income calculation works.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1How long have you owned your business?
  • 2How has the business performed over the last 12–24 months?
  • 3How do you typically pay yourself — salary, draw, or distributions?
  • 4Are your business and personal finances kept completely separate?
  • 5Do you expect this year's return to look similar, higher, or lower than last year?
  • 6What's your busiest season — is income spread out or concentrated in certain months?

INCOME DISCOVERY

Rental, VA & Support Income

DOCUMENT IT TO MAKE IT USABLE

WHY IT MATTERS

Secondary income can strengthen a file when it's properly documented and likely to continue. The right questions tell you whether it's usable.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • 1Is the rental income on a lease agreement, and are you reporting it on your taxes?
  • 2Is the VA or disability income expected to continue long-term?
  • 3Have you been receiving the child support consistently — and for how long?
  • 4Is documentation available: court orders, tax returns showing rental income, a VA award letter?
  • 5Do you have any upcoming changes to this income source?

What to Carry Into Every Call

1

The stated goal is the doorway, not the room.

Lead with the surface answer, then ask the follow-up that reveals the real driver underneath.

2

Match your questions to the income type.

W-2 is about stability; variable and self-employed are about history, trend, and documentation.

3

Listen for the trigger.

"Just exploring" almost always hides an emotional or financial event. Find it before you talk product.

4

Surface the documentation early.

Knowing what's available — returns, leases, award letters — turns a maybe into a usable file.
